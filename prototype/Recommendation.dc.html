Recommended change · Station A · open 2 days

Put a trash bin right next to the Big Belly

Pairing gives people a trash option at the same spot, so trash stops going into recycling.

Why this change

  • 64% of wrong-bin items are trash in recycling: wrappers, napkins, plastic bags. Sensor · 5 weeks
  • The nearest trash bin is about 25 m away. Site visit
  • Mistakes peak at 12–2 pm, when people leave the food court with mixed trash.
  • Worked before: the same change at Station C cut contamination by 35%; the comparison station fell 4%. See Station C →

Confidence: moderate. Strong signal here, but only 1 earlier result to learn from.
