Cellular or campus Wi-Fi uplink

Power: taps the Big Belly's solar battery; battery pack for plain bins

2 stainless clips on the lid frame. No drilling, no new task for Grounds

Status light visible only to staff during bag changes. Nothing for the public to use.

C · How hardware and software connect

Item dropped→ Camera image→ On-device: type + confidence→ Item record only→ Weekly view

Raw images deleted within 7 days (placeholder). Uncertain readings are flagged, not counted. Weighed audits stay the official result.

D · Who interacts with it

People at the bin

Nothing new to do. No screen, app or scan. They see only the privacy notice.

Grounds / custodial staff

Change bags as usual. The status light shows the module is working.

Installer

Clips it on in about 15 minutes, then runs the field-of-view and accuracy checks in Station setup.

Recycling lead (Marcus)

Never touches the module. Uses the weekly view only.
